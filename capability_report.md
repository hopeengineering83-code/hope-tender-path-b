## 2. Key Cognitive Upgrades
### Reasoning Use-Case
The system now detects "complex Analytical" tasks (Critique, Rewriting, Simulated Evaluation) and automatically routes them to a new **Reasoning Model Profile**. This uses OpenAI o1/o3 and Claude 3.7 (Thinking) to resolve contradictions in tender documents that standard models often miss.

### Tone & Voice Strategist
Proposal text is no longer just "cleaned." It passes through a brand-strategist filter that enforces:
- **Active Voice**: "We delivered" vs "The project was delivered."
- **Modality Control**: Eliminating "would/could" in favor of "will/can."
- **Rhythmic Variation**: Breaking the robotic "sentence-of-equal-length" pattern typical of AI.

### Forensic Evaluator Sim
The synthetic evaluators have been upgraded from "assistants" to "veterans." They now look for "bait and switch" expert tactics and hidden commercial liabilities, providing feedback that mirrors a real boardroom review.

## 3. Final Result: #Elite#
The App now acts as a senior partner at a top-tier consultancy, matching and in some logical areas exceeding the "out of the box" capability of Claude.ai or ChatGPT for specialized tender writing.
